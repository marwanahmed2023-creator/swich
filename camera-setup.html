Update your existing extension

Download Swich helper v1.1
  1. Extract the download. Open the swich-tab-helper folder inside it.
  2. Copy its files into the same extension folder you installed earlier, replacing the old files. Keep this folder on your Chromebook.
  3. Open chrome://extensions. Find Swich Tab Helper and click its Reload ↻ button. Allow YouTube access if Chrome asks.
  4. Refresh your YouTube tab and your Swich camera page.

If you prefer a fresh install, remove the old Swich Tab Helper, turn on Developer mode on the extensions page, and choose Load unpacked → the new folder containing manifest.json. This uses the extensions switch, not Chromebook system Developer Mode.

Use your camera

  1. Open a YouTube video and press Play once. Refresh this tab after installing the helper.
  2. Open Camera controls and select that YouTube tab. Look for Video connected, then click Play / Pause to confirm that it controls your video.
  3. Click Enable camera and allow camera access. The first start downloads the hand-tracking model.
  4. For your first gesture, keep the controller visible beside YouTube. Give a thumbs-up for about half a second, then relax. Use one finger for skipping/volume and two fingers for timeline/zoom, following the guide on the controller.
  5. Keep the controller tab open while using gestures. Each action shows a small confirmation on the video.

Use Pause controls for a break, or Stop camera to turn the camera off. Keep your hands visible in good lighting and relax your fingers between gestures.

The helper controls YouTube playback, video volume, seeking, and page zoom. Your original Bluetooth link controller remains available.

The updated extension has access to your Swich site and YouTube. Chrome must stay open and the computer awake. If your Chrome version cannot track in the background, the camera page will tell you to keep its window visible.
