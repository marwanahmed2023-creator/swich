Update your Chrome helper

Download Swich helper v1.4
  1. Extract the ZIP. Open its folders until you find manifest.json.
  2. Replace the files in your existing Swich Tab Helper folder with these new files.
  3. Open chrome://extensions and click Reload ↻ on Swich Tab Helper. This version requests access to websites so it can scroll and control media on the page you use. Allow the updated permissions if Chrome asks.
  4. Refresh the Swich controller and your media tabs.

For a new installation, turn on Developer mode on the extensions page, click Load unpacked, and select the folder directly containing manifest.json. Keep that folder in My files. Linux and Chromebook system Developer Mode are not needed.

Connect once, then use your controls

  1. Open the webpage you want to control. For video or audio, press Play once on its player.
  2. Open Camera + wire. Leave Follow the tab I use selected. You can also select a specific tab.
  3. Click Start camera and allow camera access, or Connect Bluetooth and choose your HM-10. You can use either input or both. Your existing Nano A0 / ADCTouch sketch stays the same.
  4. Use the other tab while keeping the controller open. Returning to the controller keeps the last webpage as your target.

Camera and wire controls become active when connected. Use Stop camera or Disconnect wire to stop that input. A camera permission prompt and Bluetooth device chooser are still required by Chrome.

Scroll repeatedly without lowering your hand

Down: use a slanted index finger and make a short, slow downward stroke. Aim for about 5–8% of the camera preview’s height over roughly half a second.

Up: open your hand with four fingers extended and sweep the whole hand upward quickly, about a quarter of the preview’s height in less than half a second. The controller tracks the palm for this gesture.

Each stroke scrolls roughly half a screen. Keep the same hand shape on the return: bring the slanted finger back up, or the open hand back down, then repeat. Return strokes do not scroll. You do not need to lower your hand between scrolls. On first entry or after your hand leaves the frame, hold steady briefly before a new stroke.

Keep the index finger upright for volume: move up for louder, down for quieter. Upright sideways movement skips 10 seconds. Two fingers sideways adjust the timeline. Thumbs-up plays; thumbs-down pauses.

Wire controls

Quick taps: 1 play/pause, 2 back 10 seconds, 3 forward 10 seconds, 4 open saved shortcuts. Keep gaps under 0.3 seconds and the group within 3 seconds. Brief taps now register without the old minimum press delay. The action waits 0.3 seconds for another tap; four taps act on release.

Hold for 0.7 seconds: hold increases volume; tap, then hold decreases it; two taps, then hold scrolls down; three taps, then hold scrolls up. Release to stop.

Scrolling rearms when you return your hand to its starting position. For wire holds, let go to stop. There is no mute gesture.

Other websites, audio, files, and folders

The helper supports ordinary HTML video/audio players on websites, including many embedded players. Some custom or protected players may not respond. On feeds, keep the intended video visible; the largest visible video is selected.

For media on your device, open Files & folders, choose a file or folder, select a track, and press Play. Files stay on your device. Chrome must support the file’s codec. This player remains a separate tab so the controller can follow it.

These controls affect Chrome webpages and their players. They do not control the desktop Files app, separate media applications, or system volume. Chrome settings, extension pages, and other protected browser pages cannot be controlled.

Keep Chrome running and your computer awake. The camera page tells you if your Chrome version needs its window visible beside the target page. Camera tracking happens on your device after downloading the tracking model. If extension installation is managed, your administrator must allow it.
