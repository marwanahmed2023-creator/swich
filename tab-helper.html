Switch straight to your gesture link

Install this small Chrome extension once. It opens your link as the active tab in the Chrome window you were last using and brings that window forward.

Download tab helper
  1. Extract the ZIP into a folder in My files. Keep the extracted folder after installation.
  2. Type chrome://extensions into Chrome’s address bar.
  3. Turn on Developer mode on that page. This is the extensions switch, not Chromebook system Developer Mode.
  4. Click Load unpacked. Choose the extracted swich-tab-helper folder containing manifest.json.
  5. Refresh your controller page. When it says Automatic tab switching is on, connect Bluetooth and use your gestures.

Keep the controller tab open while you browse. Your three links and two-second hold timing work as before.

Version 1.3 also supports camera-only YouTube and Facebook controls. The helper has access to your Swich site, YouTube, and Facebook; it does not request access to your browsing history.

If your school or administrator blocks extension installation, they must allow it. Chrome must remain running, and the device must be awake with Bluetooth connected.
