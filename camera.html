LIKE

Thumbs-up · play & pause

Give a thumbs-up for about half a second, then relax. Each thumbs-up acts once.

1 ↔

One finger · skip 10 seconds

Raise your index finger, pause briefly, then move it a little left or right. Relax before skipping again.

1 ↕

One finger · volume

Raise your index finger, pause briefly, then move it up or down. Relax to finish.

2 ↔

Two fingers · timeline

Raise your index and middle fingers, pause briefly, then move sideways. Bend them while still in view to set the timeline.

2 ↕

Two fingers · page zoom

Raise your index and middle fingers, pause briefly, then move up to zoom in or down to zoom out.

○

Relax between actions

Use one hand. Keep your thumb relaxed for one/two-finger controls. Small movements are enough; your first direction selects the action.
