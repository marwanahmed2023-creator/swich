LIKE

Thumbs-up · play / resume

Hold a thumbs-up briefly, then lower your hand. It plays the video; it does not like the post.

DISLIKE

Thumbs-down · pause

Hold a thumbs-down briefly, then lower your hand. It pauses the video.

1 ↔

Upright finger · skip 10 seconds

Keep your index finger upright, pause briefly, then move it a little left or right. Lower it before skipping again.

1 ↕

Upright finger · volume

Keep your index finger upright, pause briefly, then move up for louder or down for quieter. Lower it to finish.

2 ↔

Two fingers · timeline

Raise your index and middle fingers, pause briefly, then move sideways. Bend them while still in view to set the timeline.

↓ ↑

Finger down · open hand up

Down: move a slanted index finger a short distance, slowly. Up: open your hand and sweep it upward quickly, about a quarter of the camera preview’s height.

Repeat without lowering your hand: bring the slanted finger back up, or the open hand back down, then swipe again. Return movements are ignored. When first showing your hand, or returning after it leaves the frame, hold steady briefly before scrolling.

Active as soon as Bluetooth connects

Keep taps close together: gaps shorter than 0.3 seconds, within 3 seconds total. A brief touch is enough. The action follows after a short pause to check for more taps; four taps act as soon as you release. Hold the final touch for at least 0.7 seconds. Release to stop volume or scrolling.
