Six controls. Just your hands.

Use steady, deliberate movements
Ⅱ / ▶

Play & pause

Quickly pinch your thumb and index finger, then release.

−10s / +10s

Skip backward or forward

Raise your index and middle fingers. Swipe left or right, then lower them.

↕

Change volume

Pinch your thumb and middle finger. Move up or down while holding.

↔

Drag the timeline

Pinch your thumb and index finger, hold briefly, then move sideways. Release to jump.

− / +

Zoom the page

Pinch with both hands. Move them apart to zoom in, or together to zoom out.

○

Rest between gestures

Relax your fingers or lower your hands. Pause controls whenever you need a break.
